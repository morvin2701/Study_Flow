1. Information We Collect

When you use the StudyFlow app, we collect information that you provide to us directly, such as when you create an account, update your profile, or log study hours and exams. This may include:

  • Account information (name, email address, secure password)
  • Study data (study hours, focus session durations, exam schedules, work tasks)
  • Device information and usage statistics to improve app performance

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and elevate our premium services. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and securely manage your account profile
  • Seamlessly sync your study data across multiple devices
  • Deliver smart, timely reminders about upcoming exams and scheduled tasks
  • Analyze anonymized usage patterns to refine the app experience

3. Data Security

We take the security of your data extremely seriously. We deploy industry-standard end-to-end encryption protocols and maintain secure servers to protect your personal information and critical study data from unauthorized access or breaches.

4. Third-Party Services

StudyFlow is built on top-tier infrastructure and utilizes select third-party services for secure cloud hosting and reliable database management. These partners are bound by rigorous confidentiality agreements and are strictly prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.

5. Your Data Rights

You maintain full control over your data. You have the right to:

  • Request a full export of your personal and study data
  • Edit or rectify any incorrect information on your profile
  • Request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data via our Delete Account page.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. We will proactively notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy here and updating the "Effective Date."

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, our dedicated support team is ready to assist you. Please contact us at privacy@studyflowapp.com.
