Account Deletion Request

Please read carefully before proceeding. Deleting your StudyFlow account is a permanent action and will result in the loss of all your data.

Irreversible Action

This action cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

What will be deleted?

  • All your tracked study hours and logs
  • Upcoming exam schedules and deadlines
  • Work task lists and categorizations
  • Analytics, charts, and progress history
  • Your customized focus settings and preferences

If you have an active premium subscription, please ensure you cancel it through your respective app store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store) before deleting your account to avoid future charges.
